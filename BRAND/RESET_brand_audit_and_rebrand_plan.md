# RESET — brand audit and rebrand plan

Written 2026-09-26 from the public Instagram (@reset.info.nyc), the last
eleven reels, the current Partiful link, and the LIVE After Dark repo, which is
the sibling brand and the working reference for how a room at this scale runs.

Voice of this document: plain. Decisions are marked **decide**; everything
else is a recommendation you can overrule.

### What the last three years of posts say the brand is

- **A promoter/collective, not a venue.** RESET moved rooms every time:
  Speak Softly (Chelsea), The Rooftop, Selina, DROM, Mollusca. That is a
  strength. It is the thing LIVE After Dark cannot be.
- **A sound.** Afro house, deep and melodic house, plus live elements:
  Ana Fernandez (Sound of Brazil), Gifted Fire live, an all-female lineup
  (Unleash), a NYFW after party. The through-line is "house with a live pulse,
  warm not cold".
- **A face.** Andre Watson is on most of the bills and in the reels. The
  audience knows the brand through you.

### What is working

- **The eye mark.** RESET wordmark over an eye whose iris is a seed-of-life,
  with feathers hanging below. It is on every flyer from 2022 in some form and
  it is the only visual thing the audience has seen consistently. Keep it.
- **The follower base.** 2.5K real followers on a dormant account is worth
  more than a fresh handle. Do not start a new account.
- **The hashtag-free, lineup-first captions** on the 2022 posts. Good bones.

### What is broken

1. **Two names.** "RESET" on the flyers, "RESET TALENTS" on the profile,
   `@resettalents` referenced elsewhere. A guest cannot tell if this is a
   party or a booking agency. The bio says "offering services", which reads
   agency.
2. **No visual system.** Every event had its own look: tropical watercolour
   (Brazil), neon pink ring (White Party), dark jungle (Under Jungle), splashed
   portrait (NYFW), black-and-white (Unleash). Only the mark carried over.
   The grid reads as five different promoters.
3. **The profile itself.** Emoji brackets in the name, a typo in the bio
   ("Curares"), a dead link to a party that is not yours, an avatar that
   hides the logo. First impression is "abandoned".
4. **The mark is heavy.** Seed-of-life iris, radiating sound waves,
   dreamcatcher feathers, wordmark. At avatar size it turns to a smudge, the
   same problem the LIVE After Dark kit solved by cropping to LIVE only.

## 2. Positioning (the one decision everything else hangs on)

**Recommendation: RESET is the collective. "Talents" goes.**

RESET is *the party and the people who throw it*. It moves rooms, it brings a
sound, it books the lineup. Booking and DJ services still exist, but as a
line under the brand, not the name of it. Reasons:

- "RESET" is what is on every flyer people have saved. "TALENTS" is only on
  the profile.
- LIVE After Dark now exists as a fixed weekly room. RESET should be the thing
  *above* it: "RESET presents LIVE After Dark" on Saturdays, and RESET
  one-offs anywhere else. That gives both brands a job and stops them
  competing for the same followers.
- A collective can host guests, do takeovers, do a Brazil night and a Fashion
  Week after party without breaking the brand. An agency cannot.

One line, for the bio and for your own head:

> **RESET.** A New York collective throwing rooms around the city.
> Afro, deep and melodic house, with a live pulse.

**Decide:** RESET (collective) or RESET Talents (agency with events).
Everything below assumes the first.

## 4. Visual identity

### The mark

Keep the eye. Rebuild it as a **monoline** version: one stroke weight, no
gradients, no sound waves, no seed-of-life fill. The eye reads at 72px; the
detail did not. Three lockups, same as the LIVE After Dark pack:

| lockup | use |
|---|---|
| Eye only | avatar, favicon, wristband, stamp |
| RESET wordmark over the eye | flyers, stories |
| RESET wordmark, eye, feathers | print, the hero post, merch |

The feathers stay in the full lockup only. They are the part people
remember but the part that dies at small size.

### Two directions to pick from

**Direction A — Warm room.** Where the Brazil and Afro nights already were,
made consistent. Deep ground, one hot accent, film grain, the eye in bone.

| | hex | role |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | `#0B0A08` | backgrounds, a warm near-black |
| Bone | `#F2EDE8` | wordmark, names, body text |
| Ember | `#E4572E` | the accent: eye iris, dividers, dates |
| Sand | `#D9C3A3` | small accents, secondary text |

Type: a wide grotesk for RESET (the wordmark already leans this way), a
humanist sans for everything else. Grain on every ground, like the After Dark
flyers, so the two brands feel related without matching.

**Direction B — Cold signal.** The "reset" idea taken literally: a reboot,
a clean screen, a single glowing line.

| | hex | role |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | `#07090C` | backgrounds |
| Ice | `#EAF0F4` | wordmark, names |
| Signal | `#2FE6C8` | the accent: a thin neon line, the iris |
| Steel | `#8A98A6` | secondary text |

Type: a monospaced or semi-mono face for dates and metadata, a condensed
grotesk for names. No grain; sharp edges. This one is more distinct from
LIVE After Dark and more distinct from every other Afro-house promoter in
the city, but it is a bigger break from what your audience has seen.

**Decide:** A or B. My pick is **A**, because it keeps faith with the
Brazil-night warmth people came for, and it sits naturally next to LIVE
After Dark's rose-and-bone without copying it.

### Rules either way

- One accent colour. The 2022 flyers used everything.
- The mark is artwork, never re-set in type. Bake it once, like `lad7/`.
- Every night gets the same layout with only the date, room and lineup
  changed. The room's name goes in a fixed slot. That is how a moving party
  still looks like one brand.
- No stock jungle, no stock tropical. If a night has a theme, the theme is a
  colour swap and a line of copy, not a new design.

## 5. Voice

Lower than the 2022 captions, but warmer than LIVE After Dark. RESET can
have a pulse; it should not have exclamation marks.

- Short declaratives. One idea per line.
- Dates spelled out: "Saturday, October 18", never "10/18".
- Say the room and the sound. Never "secret", never "you don't want to miss".
- The lineup is the content. Names on their own lines.
- Hashtags in the first comment, not the caption.

Caption formula for a night:

```
RESET at [Room].
Saturday, October 18.

Music by
[Name]
[Name]
[Name] (live)

Doors at 10. [Neighbourhood].
RSVP at the link in bio.
```

Relaunch post, day one:

```
RESET is back.

New rooms, same sound: afro, deep and melodic house,
with a live pulse. First night announced this week.
```

## 7. Relaunch sequence

Four weeks. Nothing goes on the grid until week three.

| Week | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | You decide sections 2 and 4. I rebuild the mark as monoline, bake the three lockups, render avatar, highlight covers and a flyer template in the chosen direction. Profile housekeeping (section 3) happens now, quietly. |
| 2 | Link page built. Flyer template locked. First night booked: a room and a date. If the first RESET night is at LouLou, it is "RESET presents LIVE After Dark", which is the softest possible relaunch. |
| 3 | Relaunch post (the mark, the line). Next day, the first flyer. Story every day that week from the archive: the best 2022 clips, re-cut in the new frame. |
| 4 | The night. Recap reel within 48 hours. Second night announced in the recap caption. |

After that the cadence is the After Dark calendar (Tuesday announce, Thursday
reel, Saturday stories), which is known to be sustainable.
